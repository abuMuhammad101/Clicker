# S-8 references (Mobbin)

One search was run (the time box for research was spent on building, and the screens the search returns cost a lot of context): "Mercury banking app sign in screen", web. Mobbin returned three Mercury screens, none of them its sign-in: the home screen twice and the transactions list. Nothing below is claimed about a sign-in screen I did not see.

What the Mercury screens gave, and what was taken:

- **A welcome that is one calm line** ("Welcome, Sam"), then a row of the few actions that matter, then quiet cards. Taken: the home greeting stays the only serif line, the welcome note under it is one sentence, and "Waiting for you" is a row of quiet cards, not a dashboard. ([Mercury home](https://mobbin.com/screens/d575358b-679b-4d52-aa98-5026970291b3))
- **Counts as plain numbers**, a task count beside the sidebar item ("Tasks 1"), nothing red or pulsing. Taken: the card's count is a number in the interface font with tabular figures; no urgency styling, hidden at zero. ([Mercury home](https://mobbin.com/screens/a0539f69-cca9-4645-8f3d-aa59048efdd1))
- **A list with figures above it** ("Net change this month", "Money in", "Money out") that read before the table does: already how Clicker's summary strip works; no change.

## S-8b: the sign-in redesign

Searched on Mobbin (web): Mercury, Linear, Attio, and Stripe. Cofounder and Ramp returned nothing for a sign-in screen (the combined Stripe/Ramp/Cofounder query returned only Stripe), so nothing is claimed about them.

What each gave, and what was taken:

- **Mercury** ([log in](https://mobbin.com/screens/85da4e09-98b5-4461-b34b-3653b5083e6f)): a plain form and a quiet side panel on one card; the error sits directly under the field it is about, in a small red line with an icon, and the button stays put. Taken: the error is inline, short and iconed; a calm side panel beside the form rather than a hero. Not taken: the panel as a referral promotion, the card chrome.
- **Linear** ([log in](https://mobbin.com/screens/a44c620c-5770-47ff-88e5-e6b324298811)): one mark, one line, one primary button, nothing else on the page. Taken: restraint in the form column (title, organisation, two fields, one button, no decoration), and the wordmark as the only chrome.
- **Attio** ([sign in](https://mobbin.com/screens/a36575cf-78cd-489d-8bbc-06d8ec396dbf)): a form column on the left and a headline-plus-product panel on the right; the right side says what the product is in a large line. Taken: the split itself, the headline on the right in a larger type size, a small footer line under the form. Not taken: "Trusted by" logo rows (no real companies), the product screenshot.
- **Stripe** ([sign in](https://mobbin.com/screens/2e64feb0-9ba1-41bc-9dbc-7b8a05c174c3)): an unmistakable single primary button, and help links in the quiet footer of the form. Taken: the one wide primary button, and "help" as a quiet line below the form rather than a link beside the password. Not taken: the gradient art, "or sign in with" rows (the providers list already draws them when there are any).

What is Clicker's own: the proof strip. Three small cards built from the real chip and token styles, in the journey's words, hidden from assistive technology; the sign-in screen shows what the product does instead of a picture of it.

Departures from the references: none of them tint the panel; Clicker's is the accent-subtle token, so it is violet in light and deep indigo in dark with no new colour.
